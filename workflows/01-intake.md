# Workflow 01 — Intake

**Owner:** ACS Maturity Lead · **Time:** 30–45 minutes · **Output:** completed Profile sheet and assessment plan

## Steps

1. **Set the rules.** Remind the participants: no PHI, no passwords, no network addresses. Answers describe what is in place today.
2. **Profile.** Fill the Profile sheet of the workbook: organization type, state, beds, workforce, IT staff, primary EHR, Microsoft agreement and Microsoft 365 plan, assessment date, role of the person assessing.
3. **Scope.** Confirm which services will be assessed (all three by default) and any systems out of scope.
4. **Participants.** Identify who answers each service:
   - License Optimization — IT leader, procurement or finance, licensing partner if any.
   - App Modernization — applications lead, infrastructure lead, clinical informatics.
   - Zero Trust — security lead, infrastructure lead, compliance or privacy officer.
5. **Evidence plan.** Share `checklists/evidence-checklist.md` and agree what will be reviewed. Evidence may be shown on screen; it does not need to be copied into the workbook.
6. **Calendar.** Schedule one working session per service (60–90 minutes each) and one consolidation session.

## Exit criteria

- Profile sheet complete.
- Participants and evidence agreed for each service.
- The user confirms the plan.
